Robert Ireland



I am a fifth-year graduate student in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln working with Eloísa Grifo.
I am interested in prime characteristic commutative algebra, exploring $F$-invariants for various classes of rings. Most recently, I have been interested in the $F$-threshold and Frobenius complexity, particularly for Veronese and determinantal rings.
